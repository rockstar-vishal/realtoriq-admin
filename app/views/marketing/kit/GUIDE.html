1Get a Domain and Hosting

Use your own domain, for example {{suggested_domain}}, not a shared one. Google limits ads when many advertisers share a domain, and a developer's trademark complaint against a shared domain would stop everyone's ads. Any hosting that serves plain HTML and runs a little PHP or Node works (Hostinger, GoDaddy, cPanel, Netlify).

2Upload the Site

Upload index.html, privacy.html and the assets/ folder to your hosting's public folder, keeping assets/ next to index.html. This guide, copy.txt and creatives/ are for you and don't need uploading. Open your domain and check the page. Turn on HTTPS (free with most hosts).

Add a few lines of your own in the "About" section (why buy through you, site-visit pickup, offers you can honour). Google Ads rejects pages that are copies of other sites.

3Send Enquiries to RealtorIQ

The form posts to your own server, which forwards it to RealtorIQ. Your web developer sets this up once:

  1. In RealtorIQ, a Super Admin opens API Integrations and copies your firm's key.
  2. On your hosting, create a small script (PHP or Node) that receives the form's JSON and forwards it, adding the key:
POST {{inbound_projects_url}}
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_REALTORIQ_KEY
Content-Type: application/json

{ "mobile": "9876543210", "name": "Rahul Mehta", "email": "",
  "listing": "{{project.code}}", "enquiry_id": "web-…",
  "gclid": "…", "utm_source": "google", "utm_campaign": "…" }
  1. Put that script's URL in index.html, in the form's data-endpoint="…".
  2. Send a test enquiry. It appears in RealtorIQ under Leads within a minute, linked to {{project.name}}.

Never put the RealtorIQ key inside index.html. Anyone can read a web page's code, and could fill your leads with junk. If the key ever leaks, ask a Super Admin to make a new one.

4Track Google Ads Conversions

  1. In Google Ads, open Goals → Conversions and create a website conversion for "Enquiry" (and optionally "Call" and "WhatsApp" clicks).
  2. Paste the Google tag into index.html where it says GOOGLE ADS TAG, with your AW- ID.
  3. In the script near the bottom, replace AW-XXXXXXXXXX/ENQUIRY_LABEL (and the call and WhatsApp ones) with your conversion labels.
  4. Keep auto-tagging on in Google Ads. The page saves Google's click id (gclid) and your UTM tags with every enquiry, so RealtorIQ knows which campaign it came from.

5Stay Compliant

{{rera.authority}} (Orders 46/2023, 46B/2023 and 46C/2025) requires every ad and website by a builder or an agent to show, together in the top-right of the first screen, the project's registration number, the {{rera.authority}} website and the project QR code, with the number and website at least as large as the largest phone number or address text. This page already does that. Keep it that way if you edit the design, and do the same on every creative and post. Fines run from ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 per ad.

Show your own agent registration number ({{firm.rera_number}}) on everything you publish.

Google Ads:

  • Your ad's business name must be {{firm.name}}, never the project's or the developer's name.
  • Never call the site or yourself "official" or "authorised" unless the developer has given you that in writing.
  • You may mention {{project.name}} in ad text because this page shows prices and a way to buy. The developer can still complain to Google, and Google may then restrict that name in your ads.
  • Keep prices and offers on the page current. Promising something the page doesn't show gets ads rejected.

Privacy: the form asks for consent with an unticked box and links to privacy.html. Under India's DPDP Rules you must honour requests to stop contacting or to delete someone's details.
