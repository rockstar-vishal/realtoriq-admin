# RealtorIQ Privacy Policy

Version 1.0. Last updated 15 October 2026.

This policy explains how KGen Intelligence Private Limited ("KGen", "we", "us" or "our") processes personal data in RealtorIQ. KGen is a company incorporated in India (CIN U62011MH2026PTC475040), with its registered office at 201, Amrit Shakti, Sector R-2, Phase 2, Zara, Chandivali, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400072.

We wrote it to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it.

## 1. What this policy covers

1.1 This policy covers personal data processed through:

- the RealtorIQ registration form;
- the RealtorIQ web and mobile applications and APIs;
- broker websites published on RealtorIQ;
- KGen's internal tools for running RealtorIQ.

1.2 KGen's website privacy policy at kgen.tech/privacy covers visitors to kgen.tech. For RealtorIQ, this policy prevails where the two differ.

1.3 Words defined in the RealtorIQ Terms & Conditions have the same meaning here.

## 2. Our two roles

2.1 **KGen as Data Fiduciary.** KGen decides how and why the following personal data, called "Account Data", is processed:

- data about people who register a firm;
- data about firm owners and partners;
- data about the Authorised Users that a firm adds to its Account.

For Account Data, KGen is the Data Fiduciary, and this policy applies in full.

2.2 **KGen as Data Processor.** Brokers use RealtorIQ to manage data about their own customers: leads, prospects, buyers, tenants, landlords, sellers, and people who make enquiries on broker websites. For that data ("Customer Data"):

- the broker is the Data Fiduciary;
- KGen processes it only on the broker's behalf, as their Data Processor;
- if your data is in a broker's Account, contact that broker to exercise your rights. We will forward to the broker any request we receive, where we can identify them.

## 3. Account Data we collect

3.1 **When you register:**

- your name and designation;
- your mobile number, WhatsApp number and email address;
- your firm's name, legal name and type of entity;
- PAN and GSTIN, if you give them;
- RERA agent registration number and validity, and your RERA certificate, or your declaration that you do not hold a registration;
- a photo of your visiting card, which may show your photo, logo, address and other contact details;
- your business address, city, locality and pincode.

3.2 **When you verify your contact details:**

- records that a verification code was sent and checked. We store codes only as one-way hashes, never in readable form;
- signals from Google reCAPTCHA, which checks that a person, not a bot, is submitting the form;
- your IP address, browser and device details, and timestamps.

3.3 **When you accept the Terms:**

- the version and a digital fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the Terms and of this policy;
- the exact statement you accepted;
- the time of acceptance, your IP address and your browser details;
- your verified contact details;
- the PDF copy we email to you.

3.4 **When you use RealtorIQ:**

- the users your firm adds: their name, mobile number, email address, role and reporting lines;
- sign-in records, device names, session records and push notification subscriptions;
- records of actions taken in your Account;
- your settings and messages to our support team.

3.5 **Billing:** your plan, subscription periods, amounts, invoices and GST details.

3.6 **Integrations:** if you connect Facebook, we store your page and account identifiers, access tokens and lead form details, as needed to import your leads.

3.7 **Cookies and device storage:** we use only what RealtorIQ needs to work:

- a cookie that remembers, for a short time, which contact details you have verified while you fill in the registration form;
- sign-in tokens kept on your device by the RealtorIQ app;
- cookies that Google reCAPTCHA sets for security.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 4. Why we use Account Data

We use Account Data for the following purposes:

- to review and verify your registration, including by checking public registers such as RERA websites;
- to create, run and support your Account;
- to sign you in and keep your Account secure;
- to detect and prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate registrations and misuse of verification codes;
- to send you service, transactional, security and compliance messages;
- to bill you, and to meet our tax and accounting obligations;
- to keep a record of what you accepted, and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims;
- to comply with the law, and with orders and requests from courts and authorities;
- to improve RealtorIQ, using aggregated or de-identified data wherever possible;
- to tell you about RealtorIQ features and offers. You can opt out at any time.

## 5. Our basis for processing

5.1 We process Account Data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you register and accept the Terms.

5.2 We also process it where the DPDP Act permits processing without consent, including:

- where you provide data voluntarily for a specified purpose;
- to comply with the law or a court order;
- to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

5.3 Some data is necessary for RealtorIQ to work, such as your verified mobile number. If you do not provide it, or you later withdraw consent for it, we cannot provide RealtorIQ to you.

## 6. Who we share Account Data with

6.1 We share Account Data only as follows:

- **Our service providers.** We use them for cloud hosting and storage, SMS delivery (such as MSG91), WhatsApp delivery (such as Twilio and Meta), email delivery, bot protection (Google reCAPTCHA) and error monitoring. They act on our instructions and are bound by confidentiality.
- **Meta.** Only if you connect your Facebook pages to import leads.
- **The Network.** Other brokers and builders can see your firm's name, logo, contact details and the listings you choose to share.
- **The public.** Visitors to your broker website can see what you publish there.
- **Builders.** Builders on LaunchIQ receive the enquiries and details that you send them.
- **Advisers.** Our professional advisers and auditors, under confidentiality.
- **Authorities.** Courts, regulators and other authorities, where the law requires it.
- **A successor.** A successor business, if KGen is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

6.2 We do not sell personal data.

## 7. Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside India. We transfer personal data abroad only as the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified under it allow.

## 8. How long we keep Account Data

We keep Account Data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires:

- **Registrations you don't submit:** nothing you type into the form is saved. Only the records of verification codes are kept, as described below.
- **Registrations that are rejected, or not activated within 7 days of submission:** your user account is removed at once, so your mobile number and email address are free to register again. The rest of the registration, including the documents, the acceptance record and the PDF, is deleted 30 days later.
- **Activated registrations:** the documents are kept for as long as your Account exists, and deleted with your Account Data after it closes. The acceptance record and the PDF are kept for 3 years after your Account closes, as evidence of what was agreed, and then deleted.
- **Account Data:** kept while your Account is active, and deleted within 90 days after it closes. The exceptions are billing, tax and accounting records, which we keep for 8 years, and logs, which we keep for at least 1 year as the law requires.
- **Verification codes:** stored only as hashes, and deleted within a day of expiry.
- **Customer Data:** handled as the broker instructs, and as set out in clause 17.3 of the Terms.

## 9. How we protect Account Data

9.1 We use reasonable security safeguards, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS);
- one-way hashing of verification codes;
- private storage for documents, which only authorised staff can open;
- access limited to authorised staff;
- records of administrative actions.

9.2 No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that Account Data will never be accessed without authorisation.

9.3 If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and the affected individuals as the DPDP Act requires.

## 10. Your rights

10.1 Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

- get a summary of your personal data that we process, and of who we have shared it with;
- have your personal data corrected, completed, updated or erased;
- withdraw your consent, as easily as you gave it;
- have your grievances addressed;
- nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapacitated.

10.2 To make a request, email support@kgen.tech from your registered email address with the subject "Privacy Rights Request". We may ask you to verify your identity first. We will respond within the time the law requires.

10.3 Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place. We may still keep data where the law requires it, or where it is needed for legal claims. If you withdraw consent to data that RealtorIQ needs in order to work, we will close your Account.

## 11. Your duties

The DPDP Act also requires you to:

- comply with the law when you exercise your rights;
- not impersonate anyone;
- not suppress any material information;
- not file false or frivolous grievances;
- give only authentic information when you ask for a correction.

## 12. Children

RealtorIQ is for businesses only. Do not register if you are under 18. If we learn that we have collected data from a person under 18, we will delete it.
